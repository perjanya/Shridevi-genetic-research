# Coordinator guide

1. Open **Portfolio** and select **Add research topic**. Save a title to receive a permanent study ID. If a duplicate title is intentional, record why the studies are distinct.
2. Open **Study details** to record the research question, design, feasibility, planning wave, owner and due date. Confirm one lead and select collaborating departments. Record the PI, guide and scholar independently. Select their authorized accounts only when access has been approved.
3. Add a concept note in **Documents**. Reuse the document group name to create another version. The prior version remains available. Enter direct estimates, PI-managed cost, approved budget, commitments and spend in their separate fields. Leave unknown amounts blank.
4. In **Milestones**, expand the appropriate workflow track. Enter the effective date and evidence, then save for review. R&D or a configured role confirms the evidence and freezes completed milestones. Frozen versions can be reopened by an authorized role with a reason. Prior versions remain in **History**.
5. Record SRC and IEC submissions separately from decisions. Track the effective date, expiry and reference for applicable approvals, amendments or renewals. Complete the funding and start checklist before recording recruitment or research sample collection. The Overview and **My Actions** show workflow blockers.
6. Use **Delivery & outputs** for project-level recruitment and sample increments, reviewed lab activity, external grants and manuscripts. When a protocol changes the targets after activation, record the approved change reference. Check progress at 30, 60 and 90 days.
7. In **Reviews**, choose a reporting period and set any department, PI, wave, method or cycle filter. Draft the review, add findings and assign dated follow-ups. R&D freezes the snapshot and can draft a new version with a correction reason. Print or export the displayed report.
8. Submit feedback through the campaign form. Designated reviewers open the feedback queue to assign and resolve corrective actions. Use the status badges to find overdue items.

Record institutional project details and evidence references only. Manage patient and participant information in approved systems.
