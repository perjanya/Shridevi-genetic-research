# Role matrix

| Role | Study scope | Default workflow access | Reviews and feedback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R&D administrator | All studies and documents | Manage studies, settings and accounts. Record, confirm, freeze and reopen milestone evidence. Confirm lab activity. | View live reports. Freeze and correct reviews. Create feedback campaigns. View suppressed aggregate ratings. |
| Research coordinator | All studies and documents | Add and edit concepts, assignments, budgets, records and documents. Record milestone evidence for review. | View live reports, draft reviews and assign follow-ups. Submit feedback. |
| Principal investigator | Studies explicitly assigned to the account as PI or guide | View scoped records. Confirm endorsement, funding commitment and final report for studies assigned as PI. | Submit feedback. |
| PG guide | Studies explicitly assigned to the account as PI or guide | View scoped records. Milestone actions require custom role configuration. | Submit feedback. |
| PG scholar | Studies explicitly assigned to the account | View scoped records. | Submit feedback. |
| Departmental lead | Confirmed lead or collaborating departments matching the account's department | View scoped records. Milestone actions require custom role configuration. | Submit feedback. |
| Laboratory lead | Institutional laboratory portfolio | View studies. Record assays, quality checks and reconciliation milestones. Maintain and confirm aggregate lab records. | Submit feedback. |
| Management viewer | Institution-wide portfolio summary | View aggregate portfolio and approved reviews. | Export frozen review results and see aggregate feedback. |
| Designated feedback reviewer | Feedback workspace | Review eligible comments and create or resolve corrective actions. | View suppressed aggregate ratings and submit feedback. |

Named assignments document ownership. Account access uses separate links and an active authorized account. If the same person serves as PI and guide, use the PI account for both assignments when that person is authorized to confirm PI evidence. Custom milestone permissions replace the listed defaults for that milestone. R&D should map permissions to its written delegation policy. Record committee outcomes and references after the institutional decision.

Anonymous response records omit the respondent's account and perspective. The separate campaign redemption table limits responses to one per account, with no response ID or redemption timestamp. Reviewers see anonymous comments after the campaign reaches five anonymous submissions. Anonymous project and submission-date metadata are hidden in the reviewer UI.
